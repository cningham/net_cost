2. Common unit

$$y_{ih}=\frac{N_{ih}-N_{h}^{\text{nat}}}{\text{GDPpc}_{h,t}}$$
$$\tilde y_{ih}=\frac{y_{ih}}{s_h}$$

\(N_{ih}\): net contribution per person of origin group \(i\) in host \(h\); \(N_h^{\text{nat}}\): natives; \(t\): data year; \(s_h\): SD of \(y_{ih}\) within host \(h\).

3. Model

$$\tilde y_{ih}\sim t_{5}\!\left(\alpha_h+\lambda_h\,\bar\theta_i,\ \sigma_h\right)$$
$$\bar\theta_i=\sum_{c\in G_i}\frac{\theta_c}{|G_i|}$$
$$\theta_c=b_0+\beta_1 z^{\text{gdp}}_c+\beta_2 z^{\text{ref}}_c+\beta_3\,\text{EU}_c+\tau u_c$$
$$u_c\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$$

\(G_i\): origin countries in group \(i\) (one country, or a Dutch regional group). \(z^{\text{gdp}}\): standardised \(\log\) GDP per capita (PPP). \(z^{\text{ref}}\): standardised \(\log(1+\text{peak refugee outflow per 1{,}000})\). \(\text{EU}\): EU/EEA/CH origin.

Identification: \(\alpha_{\text{DK}}=0,\ \lambda_{\text{DK}}=1\). Priors: \(b_0,\beta_k,\alpha_h,\log\lambda_h\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\); \(\tau,\sigma_h\sim\mathcal N^{+}(0,1)\).

Inference: NUTS, 4 chains × 1,500 draws (2,500 tuning), \(\hat R\le1.05\). Score \(=100\,s_{\text{DK}}\,\theta_c\): posterior mean and 5–95% interval. US$ \(=\) score \(\times\) mean host GDP per capita (PPP 2023, US$72,435).

Modelled: origins with no own observation, \(\theta_c\) inferred from \(G_i\) and covariates.

2. Common unit

$$E_{gk}=\sum_{a,s}P_{g,a,s}\,r^{\text{ref}}_{k,a,s}$$
$$y_{ghk}=\log\frac{O_{gk}/E_{gk}}{O_{\text{nat},k}/E_{\text{nat},k}}$$
$$\text{se}_{ghk}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{O_{gk}}+\frac{1}{O_{\text{nat},k}}}$$
$$\text{RR}^{\text{adj}}=\text{RR}^{\text{crude}}\cdot\frac{\text{SIR}_g}{\text{crude}_g}$$

\(O\): offenders (or offences) of group \(g\), crime \(k\), host \(h\). \(P\): population by age \(a\) and sex \(s\). \(r^{\text{ref}}\): native or national rate. Second form where only crude counts by crime exist: factor from total offending.

3. Model

$$y_{ghk}\sim t_5\!\left(\alpha_{hk}+s_h\log\sum_c W_{gc}\,e^{\theta_{ck}},\ \sqrt{\text{se}^2+\sigma_h^2+\mathbb 1_{\text{grp}}\sigma_{\text{grp}}^2}\right)$$
$$\theta_{ck}=b_k+\lambda_k\,g_c+\varepsilon_{ck}$$
$$g_c=\beta_1 z^{\text{gdp}}_c+\beta_2 z^{\text{ref}}_c+\beta_3\,\text{EU}_c+\rho_{r(c)}+\eta_c$$

\(W_{gc}\): 1 for a country; assumed shares of migrant stock for a regional group (Finland, Spain, Basque Country). \(\alpha_{hk}=a_h+d_{hk}\): host and host × crime level (stage, definitions). \(s_h\): host spread. \(\lambda_{\text{ALL}}=1\). \(\rho\): 7 world regions. Covariates as in the fiscal tab.

Identification: \(\sum_h a_h=0,\ \sum_h d_{hk}=0,\ \sum_h\log s_h=0,\ \sum_r\rho_r=0\). Modelled rate ratio \(=e^{\theta_{ck}}\): an average host among the nine.

Priors: \(b_k,\beta\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\); \(\lambda_k\sim\text{LogNormal}(0,0.5)\); \(\sigma_\rho,\sigma_\eta,\sigma_a\sim\mathcal N^+(0,0.5)\); \(\sigma_\varepsilon,\sigma_d,\sigma_h,\sigma_{\text{grp}}\sim\mathcal N^+(0,0.3)\). NUTS, 4 chains × 1,000 draws, \(\hat R\le1.01\), 0 divergences.

Modelled: origins measured only inside a regional group, inferred from the group and covariates.

5. Host levels

\(e^{\alpha_{hk}}\): what each host records relative to the average host, all offences.

6. Validation (Spearman ρ, all offences)

7. Not harmonised

  • Socio-economic status. Age and sex only (Sweden: crude 2.4 → 1.8 after income, education, area).
  • Naturalisation. Citizenship hosts count naturalised migrants as natives.
  • Non-residents. German suspects include travelling offenders.
  • Reporting and policing. Recorded crime, not offending.
  • Ethnicity. Country of origin is not ethnicity (e.g. stateless Palestinians under Kuwait, Lebanon).
  • Sweden, France, Austria: no rates by origin and crime; not included.

8. UK ethnicity

$$\text{RR}_g=\frac{O_g}{\sum_{s,b}P_{g,s,b}\,r_{\text{WB},s,b}\,A_{g,s}/A_{\text{WB},s}}$$

\(b\): age 10–17, 18–20, 21+. \(A\): adult age index from single-year Census ages (21+). Arrests, not convictions. Ethnicity not stated for ~20% of arrests. Population fixed at Census 2021. Groups merged where police self-definition and Census categories do not match one-to-one; no group shown on its own where that happens.
